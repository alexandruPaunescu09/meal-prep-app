WEEK 1 PILOT CHECKLIST
======================

How to use this in macOS Notes:
1. Open Notes, create a new note
2. Paste the contents of this file
3. Select all the lines starting with "*"
4. Format menu -> Checklist (Shift+Cmd+L)
5. Lines become tickable checkboxes


WEEK 0 - FOUNDATION (Mon-Wed before week 1)
--------------------------------------------
* Client intake: agree on calorie targets + macro splits + fiber per person
* Confirm pricing: ingredient cost only, no markup, no labor (verbal agreement)
* Confirm logistics: Mon/Wed/Fri delivery time + handoff location
* Confirm dietary restrictions/allergies, write into clients record in app


WEEK 0 - MENU & APP PREP (Thu-Fri)
----------------------------------
* Build week 1 menu: 18 meal-slots (6 days x 3 meals), repeats allowed
* Verify each recipe: ingredients exist, prices current, container assigned, portions correct
* Create Plan A in app for person 1 (set markup_multiplier = 1.0)
* Create Plan B in app for person 2 (set markup_multiplier = 1.0)
* Validate daily totals against each person's calorie + macro + fiber targets
* Verify prep rules cover this menu (beans, lentils, eggs, marinated proteins)


WEEK 0 - SHOPPING & PREP (Saturday)
-----------------------------------
* Generate shopping list from both meal plans, combine into one list
* Split list by store (Freshful vs Lidl)
* Place Freshful order with Saturday-morning or Friday-evening slot
* Lidl run Saturday afternoon for fresh meat + picky produce
* Container check: 24 clean and dry, lids match, replace cracked
* Stock rescue pantry: oats, fruit, nut butter, eggs
* Saturday advance prep per app's prep tasks (soak beans, marinate, wash greens)


SUNDAY - COOK DAY 1 (for Mon+Tue food)
--------------------------------------
* Lidl top-up if needed
* Final check: ingredients staged, prep tasks done, kitchen clean
* Mise en place: pull all ingredients, weigh per recipe, stage in bowls
* Start anything slow first (beans, oven preheat, stews)
* Work through stovetop dishes in sequence
* Spread cooked food on sheet pans, LIDS OFF for cooling
* Cool to <4C (1-2 hours) before sealing in glass
* Wash dishes during cooling phase
* Portion into glass containers: Plan A for person 1, Plan B for person 2
* Apply post-it labels: name + meal + day (e.g. "Ana - Mon Lunch")
* Stack in fridge overnight, coldest shelf
* Confirm Monday delivery time with client (text)
* Pre-stage delivery bag at door, ice packs in freezer


MONDAY - DELIVERY 1
-------------------
* Load food into insulated bag with ice packs
* Drive to client, ~5-min handoff (NO empties to collect - first delivery)
* Log delivery #1 in app (sent quantities, returned = 0)
* Capture any casual feedback in phone note


TUESDAY - COOK DAY 2 (for Wed+Thu food)
---------------------------------------
* Same checklist as Sunday cook day
* Use Mon feedback for portion-size tweaks (don't change menu, just portions)


WEDNESDAY - DELIVERY 2 + FIRST RETURNS
--------------------------------------
* Load Wed+Thu food into insulated bag
* At handoff: deliver food, COLLECT empties from Monday delivery
* Quick chat: how was Mon+Tue? what hit, what didn't?
* Log delivery #2 in app (sent + returned)
* CRITICAL: are all 12 Monday containers back?
* Inspect returns, note anything not rinsed (gentle - don't make it a thing)
* Wash + dry containers (need them ready for Thursday cook)


THURSDAY - COOK DAY 3 (for Fri+Sat food)
----------------------------------------
* Same checklist as Sunday/Tuesday cook day
* Containers used = the just-washed Monday set


FRIDAY - DELIVERY 3
-------------------
* Deliver Fri+Sat food, collect empties from Wednesday delivery
* Log delivery #3 in app
* Wash returned containers (ready for Sunday's week-2 cook)


SATURDAY - WEEK 1 CLOSE-OUT
---------------------------
* End-of-week debrief with both people (30-45 min, in person or video)
* Walk through every meal, ask explicit questions:
    - what would you change?
    - anything you didn't finish?
    - portion sizes too big/small?
    - any meal you'd remove from rotation?
* Capture answers in writing
* Settle ingredient cost (use app's calculated total), send invoice, get paid
* Inventory check: all Friday containers back? anything broken? rescue pantry depleted?
* Decisions for week 2: pricing? cadence? menu changes?


POLICIES (reference, not checkbox)
==================================

FOOD SAFETY
- Cool to <4C before sealing glass containers (lids off, 1-2 hours)
- Never warm-pack stacked sealed containers
- Deliveries always cold with ice packs
- Late cook session = deliver next day, not same evening

LABELING
- Post-it on lid: name + meal + day (e.g. "Ana - Mon Lunch")
- Reapply each cycle, don't try to make labels survive washing

CONTAINERS (pilot)
- 2 sets owned, no buffer
- Shortage even ONCE in week 1-2 = buy 3rd set before week 3

PRICING (week 1 only)
- Ingredient cost only, markup_multiplier = 1.0
- You absorb partial-package waste
- Week 2+ requires real pricing conversation

FAILURE HANDLING
- Cook fails -> rescue meal (oats + fruit + nut butter or eggs)
- Container breaks in transport -> refund affected meal, no remake
- You're sick -> skip meals, eat the loss (pilot only)
- Client cancels mid-week -> eat/freeze cooked food, no charge for future

RESCUE PANTRY (always on hand)
- Rolled oats
- Fruit (banana, apples)
- Nut butter
- Eggs

FEEDBACK CAPTURE
- In-week: one phone note, one line per comment
- End-of-week: Saturday debrief, structured questions
- Friend-clients under-report - ask explicit questions

APP USAGE
- Meal plans = source of truth, don't change mid-week
- Prep page: check off tasks as you do them
- Containers/deliveries page: log every delivery (sent + returned)
- Shopping list: generate Saturday week 0 only
